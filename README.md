### Seppo Takalo

Principal R&D Engineer at [Nordic Semiconductor](https://www.nordicsemi.com), Oulu, Finland.

I build the software that connects small devices to cellular networks.
My focus is the [Zephyr RTOS](https://zephyrproject.org/) and the [nRF Connect SDK](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Development-software/nrf-connect-sdk),
with deep experience in cellular IoT: LTE-M/NB-IoT, PPP and CMUX, AT-command interfaces and LwM2M device management.

#### What I work on

* **Zephyr cellular modem stack:** modem drivers, CMUX, PPP and AT-command pipes, including the nRF93M1 and nRF91 Serial Modem drivers.
* **nRF93M1:** board support and samples in Zephyr and the nRF Connect SDK.
* **[Serial Modem](https://github.com/nrfconnect/ncs-serial-modem):** regular contributor, focusing on CMUX, PPP and robustness.
* **Low-power cellular:** author of the white paper
  [Ultra low power PPP connection using nRF9151](https://response.nordicsemi.com/ultra-low-power-ppp-connection-using-nrf9151).
  * See [Nordic Semiconductor/Cellular IoT/White papers](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Wireless/Low-power-cellular-IoT/White-Papers).
* **LwM2M:** I founded and led the team that was the main contributor to the Zephyr LwM2M stack, including LwM2M v1.1 support.
